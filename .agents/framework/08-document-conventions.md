# 08 Document Conventions

## HLD naming

- `docs/hld/HLD-001-<slug>.md`

## Story naming

- `docs/stories/STORY-001-<slug>.md`

## ADR naming

- `docs/adr/ADR-001-<slug>.md`

## Status vocabulary

Use only these story statuses:

- `draft`
- `ready`
- `in-red`
- `in-green`
- `in-refactor`
- `in-review`
- `done`
- `blocked`
- `deferred`

## Completion notes

Every completed story should include:

- what changed
- what tests cover it
- what remains out of scope
